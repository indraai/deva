## 1. Rooted in Vedic teachings

The Deva Core is built on Vedic teachings. Its agents are Devas, and as they work they record their actions, states, intents and beliefs, following the Vedic idea that every action through mind, speech and body carries a consequence. It is also the base that Quinn Michaels builds his own system from, so the Deva Core sits behind everything else in that system.

The license follows the same roots. It is grounded in Vedic Law and works under a threefold order:

1. **Vedic Law** sets right action, rightful ownership and the measure of consequences.
2. **The Constitution of the United States** gives the secular framework, protecting the free exercise of religion and due process.
3. **Owner Authority** rests with Quinn Michaels, whose signature and consent are required for any valid license.

All three must be satisfied for use to be lawful.

## 2. The path of a teacher, not a wall

The VLA is not meant to keep people away. It follows the path of a Vedic teacher and student:

- **Ask permission.** A student does not take a teaching without first asking the teacher. In the same way, using the Deva Core starts with asking Quinn Michaels for a license.
- **Make a proper offering.** In the Vedic tradition, a student gives a proper offering to the teacher in return for the teaching. For the Deva Core, that offering is agreed when the license is set up, and it supports the work going forward.
- **Build a community around the teachings.** Once the relationship is in place, the goal is a community of people and teams who learn, build and grow with the Deva Core together, with the teacher's guidance.

That is why the agreement protects the work by default. Without a license, the Deva Core may not be modified, redistributed, sub-licensed, reverse engineered or shared, and using it without permission is treated as taking what has not been given. With a license, the door is open.

The agreement also sets these limits, which apply to every license:

- Use is confined to the United States of America.
- Official, government and commercial use needs express authorization from the Owner with verified legal signatures.
- The Owner may revoke access at any time.
- The Software is provided as-is, without warranty.

## 3. How to ask for a license

Every license starts the same way: write to Quinn Michaels at [quinn@indra.ai](mailto:quinn@indra.ai) and tell him who you are and how you want to use the Deva Core. The sections below say what to include for each kind of use.

### Personal use

For individuals learning, experimenting or building their own projects. Include your name, what you want to build, and whether it will stay private or be shared with anyone else.

### Corporate use

For companies and teams building products or internal systems. Commercial use needs express authorization with verified legal signatures. Include your company name, the people responsible, the product or system you plan to build, how many Devas or deployments you expect, and whether you want consulting help from Quinn to design and build it.

### Government use

For any official or government body. Government use needs express authorization with verified legal signatures. Include the agency or office, the official responsible, the purpose of the system, and where it will run.

### Educational use

For schools, universities, teachers and students. Include the institution, the course or program, the number of students, and how the Deva Core will be taught or used.

### Military use

Military use is official government use under the agreement, so it needs express authorization with verified legal signatures. Include the branch or unit, the authorized official, the purpose of the system, and where it will run.
